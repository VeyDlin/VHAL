# AT+TEMP? times out at 100 ms and is drained: the drain sends AT. The module then answers the late request with OK, and the drain's AT
# with its own OK. A late OK is the same line as the drain's OK, so the drain waits for the second OK. AT+RUN is sent after that, and
# its ERROR is its own status, not the drain's OK.
> AT+TEMP?
@ 100
> AT
< OK
< OK
> AT+RUN
< ERROR
